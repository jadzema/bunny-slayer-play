Privacy Policy — Bunny Slayer 3D

Effective date: October 2, 2026

Bunny Slayer 3D ("the game") is a single-player video game. This policy explains what data the game does and does not handle. The short version: the game does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

Information we collect

None. The game does not ask for, collect, or transmit any personal information. There are no user accounts, no sign-in, and no registration.

Game save data

Your game progress (such as the level you have reached and your saved slots) is stored locally on your own device, in the app's local storage. This data never leaves your device, is never sent to us or anyone else, and is removed if you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Advertising, analytics, and third parties

The game contains no advertising, no third-party analytics, and no third-party tracking or advertising SDKs. We do not share or sell any data, because we do not collect any.

Network use

The game runs entirely on your device and does not require an internet connection to play.

Children

The game depicts stylized, cartoon-style action violence and is not directed at young children. It is intended for a teen and older audience. We do not knowingly collect any information from children (we do not collect information from anyone).

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this page with a new effective date.

Contact
